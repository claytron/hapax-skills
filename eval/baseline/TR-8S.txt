[COMMENT]
Roland TR-8S (Version 1.10 MIDI chart / manual v3.00). Default channel 10 (UTILITY:MIDI:Pattern Ch).
Hapax allows 8 drum lanes; TR-8S has 11 instruments. Omitted: MT 47, CC 49, RC 51.
Alt notes: BD 35, SD 40, LT 41, MT 45, HT 48, RS 56, HC 54, CH 44, OH 55, CC 61, RC 63.
Note numbers are configurable on UTILITY:MIDI:Inst Note.
PC 0-127 selects kits on Kit Ch (Rx Prog Chg must be ON).
CC 14 and 70 (Auto Fill In) are transmit-only and not listed.
[/COMMENT]
